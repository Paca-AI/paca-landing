Why Paca

Not a chatbot bolted on the side. A teammate on the board.

Jira gives you a backlog. ClickUp gives you automations. Monday gives you dashboards. Paca gives your AI agents a seat at the table.

Teammates, not chatbots

Agents are assigned to sprints, appear on the Scrumban board next to humans, and update task status in real time. No separate "AI workspace."

Probe · sense · respond

Rooted in the Cynefin framework: complex work needs adaptive teams, not rigid pipelines. Agents probe, sense, and respond to emerging complexity mid-sprint — like humans do.

Grounded in your docs

BDD scenarios and living System Design Documents keep every agent contextually anchored — so output matches how your product actually works.

ばか · THE "PACA" STORY

The name is a pun on the Japanese word "Baka" — silly. Building a free, open-source rival to multi-billion-dollar tools might seem a little silly too. We think it's worth being foolish about.

Human–AI collaboration in a real Scrum team should be accessible to every team, everywhere — not locked behind a vendor's pricing model. 🦙
